Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short: Face Off Arcade does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app stores

The player names you type, the Boost settings and your last chosen game category are saved only on your device, so they're still there next time. They never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No network requests of its own and no third-party code that collects data.

Purchases

The optional "Unlock All Games" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app only receives Apple's confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the games.

Children

The app is made for families to play together. Because it collects no data, no information about children is collected. Purchases are behind a parental gate.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
