Frequently asked questions

How do two people play on one screen?
Lay the iPad or iPhone flat between you. Each player owns the half of the screen nearest to them, and their controls and text face them.

What does Boost ⚡ do?
Boost gives one player a head start: a bigger paddle, more lives or a shorter course, depending on the game. Each game card says what Boost changes. It's set per player on the home screen.

I bought "Unlock All Games" but the games are still locked.
Open any locked game, tap Restore purchases, and sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy. Purchases made by a family organizer are shared automatically through Family Sharing.

Why does the app ask a maths question before buying?
That's a parental gate, so children can't start a purchase by accident.

How do I leave a game?
Press and hold the home button on the left edge of the screen.
